1. Kamakura

The seated Buddha

Great Buddha of Kamakura

The Great Buddha of Kamakura, also known as Kamakura Daibutsu, is a monumental outdoor bronze statue of Amitābha Buddha located on the grounds of Kotoku-in Temple in Kamakura

Location

Daibutsu Kamakura

Significance:
The Great Buddha is a designated National Treasure of Japan and a significant cultural icon, drawing millions of visitors annually. It is a symbol of peace and enlightenment.

Height and Weight:
 Including its base, the statue stands at 13.35 meters (43.8 ft) tall and weighs approximately 93 to 121 tons (sources vary slightly). It is the second tallest bronze Buddha statue in Japan.

Giant Straw Sandals

Hanging to the right of the Buddha, these were donated by children wishing for the Buddha to walk across Japan and bring happiness. The tradition continues to this day

Interior and architechture

you can also visit the inside of the Buddha statue and witness the bronze masterpiece reveals a fascinating view of ancient construction techniques, you can see the thick bronze panels joined together with massive bolts and rivets.

Food

Make sure to try the Yaki-dango,Soft rice dumplings grilled on skewers and brushed with sweet soy sauce — often sold at temple approach streets.
