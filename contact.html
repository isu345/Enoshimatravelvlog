Enoshima Beach

Enoshima's endless blue

Introduction

Enoshima Beach, officially Yuigahama Beach and Zaimokuza Beach when viewed collectively with the nearby stretches of sand, is a popular seaside escape near Enoshima Island. It's known for its broad sandy shores, vibrant summer atmosphere, and recreational activities like swimming and sunbathing. The beach offers excellent views of the island, and on clear days, even Mount Fuji

Transport

The Enoden train line is a charming and convenient way to get between Kamakura and Enoshima, offering picturesque views along the coast.

Small Cafes

discover the charming small cafes dotting the area around Enoshima Beach. These cozy spots offer the perfect break for a freshly brewed coffee, a cool drink, or a light snack, all with a unique local ambiance
